# QUORIX

AI-Powered Procurement Intelligence & Bidder Evaluation Platform

AI Decision Intelligence for Transparent & Defensible Procurement

Created by Riya Chauhan

---

## Problem Statement

Traditional procurement evaluation is slow, manual, inconsistent, and difficult to audit. Organizations struggle to compare bidders fairly while maintaining transparency, compliance, and defensible decision-making.

---

## Overview

QUORIX is an AI-assisted procurement intelligence platform designed to streamline vendor evaluation, improve transparency, and provide audit-ready procurement decision support.

The platform enables organizations to:
- Extract bidder information from procurement documents
- Evaluate vendor eligibility automatically
- Compare multiple bidders intelligently
- Generate explainable recommendations
- Maintain audit-ready procurement records

---

## Key Features

- Procurement document extraction
- AI-assisted bidder evaluation
- Weighted scoring engine
- Multi-bidder comparison
- Audit trail logging

QUORIX combines AI-assisted extraction, explainable scoring, policy-driven evaluation, and audit-ready procurement intelligence into one unified decision support platform.
